# Todd Memorial Chapel — Concept Redesign

A modern, mobile-first redesign of [toddmemorialchapel.com](http://www.toddmemorialchapel.com/), prepared by **[Tribuary](https://tribuary.com)** as a foundation-first preview for the Todd family.

> **Tribuary** — the operating system for modern funeral homes.

## What this is

A seven-page concept redesign of the Todd Memorial Chapel website, built using the chapel's own content, photographs, and family history. The redesign demonstrates what's possible when 118 years of trust meets a modern digital foundation:

- A mobile-responsive site that works on every device
- A real online inquiry form for both immediate-need and pre-planning families
- A modern obituary and tribute system
- Clear pre-need planning, aftercare, and grief resource sections
- Prominent display of the chapel's 4.8★ Google rating
- Two-location awareness (Pomona since 1907, Claremont since 1954)

## Pages

- **`index.html`** — Home
- **`about.html`** — Our Family · five-generation history with leadership photos
- **`services.html`** — Traditional, graveside, cremation, transportation, pre-need
- **`pricelist.html`** — General Price List (illustrative)
- **`obituaries.html`** — Modern obituary feed and tribute system
- **`resources.html`** — Veterans benefits, Social Security, grief support, local information
- **`contact.html`** — Both locations, full contact, directions, embedded map, inquiry form

## Content & photography

Family history, leadership photographs, chapel imagery, and service language come from the chapel's existing public materials. Pricing is illustrative; live pricing will reflect the chapel's current FTC General Price List.

## Tech notes

- Single static stylesheet (`assets/styles.css`) — no build step
- Google Fonts: Cormorant Garamond + Inter
- Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, fully accessible
- Mobile-first, sticky 24-hour availability bar, floating call CTA on phones
