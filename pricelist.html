General Price List

Honest pricing, itemized in plain English.

Affordable, transparent pricing has been the Todd family commitment since 1907. Every service we offer, every casket we sell, every fee we charge — listed openly. You see every line. You choose every option.

This list, in compliance with the FTC Funeral Rule and California state law, presents itemized prices for goods and services we offer. Prices are subject to change. The goods and services shown below are those we can provide to our customers. You may choose only the items you desire. However, any funeral arrangements you select will include a charge for our basic services and overhead. If legal or other requirements mean you must buy any items you did not specifically ask for, we will explain the reason in writing on the statement we provide describing the funeral goods and services you selected.
