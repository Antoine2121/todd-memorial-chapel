After a loved one passes, families face a stack of decisions and forms — VA benefits, Social Security, certified copies, insurance. This page is where we put everything we have learned, in one place, for you.

For Veterans & Families

Veterans Burial Benefits.

If your loved one served in the United States Armed Forces, they may be entitled to a range of burial benefits — and we walk you through every one of them.

  • ✓
    Burial in any national cemetery at no cost to the family, with full military honors when requested.
  • ✓
    Government-furnished headstone or marker for any eligible veteran, even when burial occurs in a private cemetery.
  • ✓
    Presidential Memorial Certificate — a signed certificate honoring the deceased's service, available to family members.
  • ✓
    Burial flag at no cost — given to the next of kin or used to drape the casket.
  • ✓
    Reimbursement of burial expenses for service-connected and certain non-service-connected deaths, up to current VA limits.

For Surviving Spouses & Dependents

Social Security Survivor Benefits.

Social Security pays a one-time death benefit and may pay ongoing monthly survivor benefits to a surviving spouse and minor children. The rules are not complicated, but they are not always clearly explained — and we are happy to walk you through them.

  • ✓
    $255 lump-sum death benefit for an eligible surviving spouse or dependent child.
  • ✓
    Monthly survivor benefits for a spouse age 60+ (50+ if disabled), or for minor or disabled children.
  • ✓
    Mother's or father's benefits for a surviving spouse caring for a child under 16.
  • ✓
    Notification on your behalf — we typically report the death to Social Security as part of our service to you, so the agency knows immediately.
