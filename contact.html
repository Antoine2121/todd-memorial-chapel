Driving Directions

Finding the Pomona Chapel.

Whether you are coming from the west on the 10, the east, or somewhere in between, here is how to get to 570 North Garey Avenue.

From Los Angeles

Take Interstate 10 East. Exit at Garey Avenue. Turn south (right) onto Garey. Continue approximately one mile — Todd Memorial Chapel is on the east side of the street.

Open in Google Maps →

From San Bernardino

Take Interstate 10 West. Exit at Garey Avenue. Turn south (left) onto Garey. Continue approximately one mile — Todd Memorial Chapel is on the east side of the street.

Open in Google Maps →

From LAX or Ontario Airport

From LAX: take I-105 East, then I-605 North, then I-10 East — exit Garey Ave. From Ontario (ONT): take I-10 West about 12 minutes — exit Garey Ave.
