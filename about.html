Our Family · Since 1907

One family, five generations, one promise.

To care for the families of the Pomona Valley as if they were our own — kept by the Todd family since 1907, and by every generation since.

Independent on Purpose

A Pomona Valley institution.

Founded in April of 1907 by Walter B. Todd, this funeral home built its reputation on hospitality and a deep love for the community we serve. Walter integrated the business into civic life, and the family has been doing the same ever since.

While much of the funeral industry has consolidated under corporate ownership, we have stayed family-owned by choice — because the way you care for a family in the worst week of their life shouldn't be a quarterly metric. We serve every faith, every cemetery in the area, and every tradition our families ask us to honor.

Our History

A century and counting.

From a single chapel in 1907 to two locations and five generations — the milestones of a family business kept in the family.

1907

Walter B. Todd opens Todd Memorial Chapel.

Founder Walter B. Todd opens the funeral home in Pomona in April of 1907, building its reputation on hospitality and deep involvement in the local community.

1933

The second generation steps in.

When Walter passes in 1933, his son John R. Todd assumes leadership of the family business — a transition that would set the pattern for every generation since.

1947

Three names continue the family tradition.

Leadership transitions to Richard K. Todd, John W. Todd, and Lawrence C. Smith — the third generation continuing the family's stewardship of the business.

1954

Expansion to Claremont.

Todd Memorial Chapel opens a second location in Claremont to better serve families in the eastern Pomona Valley — same family, same care, two chapels.

Today

The fourth and fifth generations carry the name forward.

Today the family business is led by John R. Todd in the fourth generation, alongside Brian E. Pewe and Keith E. Pewe in the fifth. Independent. Family-owned. By choice.
