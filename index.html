Our Family

A Pomona Valley institution, kept independent on purpose.

Founded in April of 1907 by Walter B. Todd, this funeral home built its reputation on hospitality and a deep love for the community we serve. Five generations later, that has not changed. Our family is now led by John R. Todd, fourth generation, alongside Brian E. Pewe and Keith E. Pewe in the fifth.

We assist with burial, entombment, cremation, and out-of-state and international transportation. We serve every faith, every cemetery in the area, and every tradition our families ask us to honor — Catholic, Christian, Jewish, military, secular, or anything in between.
