Each obituary at Todd Memorial Chapel is written by the family, with our help. Photos, music, the people they loved, the things they cared about — all of it goes on the tribute page that lives online for as long as the family wants it to.

When you are arranging a service with us, our family will sit with yours and help write the obituary in a way that honors who they were. There is no template, no rush, and no word count.
